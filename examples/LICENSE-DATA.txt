They are four 20 s overhead recordings of broiler chickens, together with the
bounding boxes and track identities a YOLO11x detector and a ByteTrack tracker
produced for them. They come from a trial at the Texas A&M University Poultry
Science Center approved by the Texas A&M University Institutional Animal Care
and Use Committee under protocol IACUC 2024-0108.

If you use them, please cite the study they come from. See CITATION.cff in the
repository root.
